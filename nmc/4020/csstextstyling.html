Let Me Show You something fun!

When you use CSS, you can align your text in different ways! This text has been aligned to start on the right and move to the left as text gets added. It also has a white space property(pre-line) added onto it that changes the way new lines are made. Lastly, we have added font sizes to it that change it to be 1.5 times the size of the starting font size and have added spaces between the lines. Pretty cool right?!

This text on the other hand doesn't have much going on. It, alongside its family(h1,h3), have been aligned to the center of the page! What makes this one unique is that it is 3.5 times the size of the default font size, placing its size directly in the middle of h1 and h3.
